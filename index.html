About Rabt

“Rabt” means connection.

رابط (Rabt) is the Arabic word for connection, and that is what we build: connections between people, processes and data.

Based in Arizona, Rabt is a SaaS company serving organizations in English and Arabic, with a focus on practical technology that delivers measurable results.
